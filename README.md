## Hey, I'm Lingam Charan 👋

**Final-year Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design & Technology) student · Founder @TapeoutX**

I'm pursuing VLSI, and I love building and shipping things. Most of what you see here started as *"I wonder if I can make that."*

- 🎓 **B.Tech in Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design & Technology)**, NRI Institute of Technology · Class of 2027
- 🔬 Working on **digital design**: RTL in Verilog/SystemVerilog, verification, and **RTL→GDS with OpenLane on Sky130**
- 🚀 Founder of **[TapeoutX](https://tapeoutx.vercel.app/)**, a community where VLSI students figure it out together
- 🎥 Documented a **120-day VLSI challenge** on Instagram ([@the_charan_way](https://www.instagram.com/the_charan_way/))
- 🛠️ Builds and ships side projects: web apps, AI tools and whatever else catches my curiosity

## 🔬 Silicon

| Project | What it is | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| **[inverter-RTL-GDS](https://github.com/charan-lingam/inverter-RTL-GDS)** | Full **RTL → GDSII** flow for an inverter cell on the **SkyWater 130nm** PDK, from Verilog to a final layout viewed in KLayout | Verilog · OpenLane · Sky130 · KLayout |
| **[FIFO](https://github.com/charan-lingam/FIFO)** | Synthesizable 8-bit × 16 synchronous FIFO with a circular buffer, full/empty flags and simultaneous read and write. Verified with a SystemVerilog testbench | Verilog · SystemVerilog |
| **[FILO](https://github.com/charan-lingam/FILO)** | Synthesizable 8-bit × 16 hardware stack (LIFO) with push/pop, an occupancy counter and full/empty flags, plus a testbench | Verilog |

## 🧠 My AI OS

I run my ventures on a personal AI operating system built on Claude Code. I used [Nate Herk's AIS-OS](https://github.com/nateherkai/AIS-OS) as the reference to build my own, then added my own agents:

- **Community agent:** reads the TapeoutX Discord daily and drafts personal DMs and conversation starters
- **Sales agent:** qualifies leads, follows up and drafts proposals on a daily schedule
- **Writing, PM and design skills:** emails in my voice, a self-learning product notebook, and on-demand logos and posters

## 🚀 Currently building: TapeoutX

**[TapeoutX](https://tapeoutx.vercel.app/)**: *Tapeout is the finish line. TapeoutX is the journey.*

Most VLSI students, especially outside the big colleges, figure it out alone, with no seniors, no network and no direction. I started TapeoutX so they don't have to. It's a community of VLSI students offering learning paths, industry insights, real student journeys and resources.
